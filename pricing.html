Financing FAQ

Applying for CareCredit does involve a hard credit inquiry, which may temporarily affect your score by a few points. However, if you're approved and manage your account responsibly, your credit score may improve over time. Many patients are approved even with less-than-perfect credit.

For CareCredit accounts, a missed payment may result in interest charges and late fees per the cardholder agreement. For our in-house payment plans, please contact our billing team immediately if you anticipate difficulty — we will work with you to adjust your payment schedule.

Yes! We accept HSA (Health Savings Account) and FSA (Flexible Spending Account) cards for most dental treatments, including preventive care, fillings, crowns, orthodontics, and medically necessary procedures. Note that purely cosmetic treatments like whitening are typically not FSA/HSA eligible.

For large treatment plans (typically $2,000+), we may offer a courtesy discount for payment in full at time of service. Please speak with our financial coordinator to discuss your specific treatment plan and available courtesy adjustments.
