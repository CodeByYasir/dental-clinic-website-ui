Getting Here

Directions & Parking

From the North

Take Interstate 95 South to Exit 12A. Turn left on Main Boulevard, continue for 2 miles. Turn right onto Smile Avenue. Clinic is in the medical plaza on the right, Suite 200.

From the South

Take Interstate 95 North to Exit 11. Merge onto Downtown Expressway. Take the 5th Ave exit and head north for 3 blocks. Turn left on Smile Avenue — clinic is on the left in the glass building.

From the East

Head west on Route 9 until you reach Downtown. At the intersection of Route 9 and Central Ave, turn right. Take Central Ave north for 4 blocks, then turn left on Smile Avenue.

From the West

Take the Westside Expressway east. Exit at Midtown Connector. Head east on Midtown Connector for 1.5 miles, turn right on 3rd Ave, then left on Smile Avenue at the next light.

Parking

Free dedicated patient parking in our building lot (enter from Smile Avenue). Additional street parking available on 2nd Ave and Central Ave. Parking garage across the street at $5/hour.

Public Transit

Subway: A/C/E trains to 34th Street, 5-minute walk north. Bus: M20 and M11 stop directly in front of the building. The Q32 bus stops 1 block away on 3rd Ave.

From the North

Head south on Riverside Drive. At 72nd Street turn east. Continue 2 blocks to Bright Street. Turn right — Unit B is the second entrance on the left with teal awning.

From the South

Take the West Side Highway northbound. Exit at 57th Street. Head east to Columbus Ave, turn north and continue 8 blocks. Turn left on Bright Street — clinic Unit B is mid-block.

From Midtown

Head west on 65th Street from Central Park. Continue past Columbus Avenue to Bright Street. Turn right at Bright Street — clinic is on the left, look for the teal BrightSmile sign.

From Central Park West

Take Central Park West south to 66th Street. Turn right (west) on 66th Street. At the 3rd intersection turn left. Bright Street is 1 block ahead — clinic Unit B is the teal-awning storefront.

Parking

Validated parking at the Bright Street Garage (enter from 65th St) — 2 hours free with appointment. Street parking available on Bright Street and side streets. Note alternate side parking rules.

Public Transit

Subway: 1 train to 66th Street/Lincoln Center, 3-minute walk west. Bus: M10 and M72 stop half a block from the clinic. Citi Bike station on the corner of Bright Street and Columbus.
