Gum & Periodontal Care

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile

Gum disease affects nearly half of adults over 30 and is a leading cause of tooth loss. Our periodontal team offers comprehensive gum disease treatment — from gentle deep cleanings to advanced gum therapy — to protect both your oral and overall health.

Signs You May Need Gum Care

  • Bleeding when brushing or flossing
  • Swollen, red, or tender gums
  • Receding gum line
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Loose or shifting teeth
  • Pain when chewing
🌿
